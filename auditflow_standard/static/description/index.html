Edition and scope

Standard Edition. AuditFlow Standard covers internal audit operations, review results, follow-up, readiness checks, corrective actions, and KPI cards. It is not a certified GRC platform, and it does not include control testing, automated test execution, or a separate risk-management product.

Features

What the Standard edition includes.

IconFeatureSummary
📅 Audit operation lifecycle Move an audit operation through Draft, Fieldwork, Reporting, Issued, Closed, and Cancelled. Record the entity under review, audit team, lead reviewer, dates, scope, and objectives. Key changes are tracked on the chatter.
📝 Review results Record severity, execution status, accountable owner, target execution date, impact, likelihood, management response, and attachments. Risk Rating is calculated from Impact and Likelihood. Severity labels are Low, Medium, High, and High Risk.
✅ Report readiness Check the entity under review, lead reviewer, dates, scope, objectives, and open review-result fields before issuing a report. Open review results are checked for an action plan, target date, corrective action owner, and management response. An open or in-review review result whose severity is High Risk must have a Corrective Action (CAPA). Delayed corrective actions are also reported.
⚠️ Issue report warning Issue Report from Reporting opens the Issue Report Warning wizard when checks fail. Cancel leaves the operation in Reporting. Force Issue Anyway sets the state to Issued and overrides the checks. A ready operation is issued directly.
⏰ Overdue review results Review results that are still open or in review and past the target execution date are marked overdue. A configurable due-soon window flags dates that are approaching. Terminal statuses are not treated as overdue.
📊 Executive dashboard KPI cards for engagements, review results, risk level, and corrective actions. The selected card opens a Bar Chart, Trend, or Distribution chart.
🔧 Corrective Actions (CAPA) Create corrective actions from review results and follow the stored states draft, assigned, in progress, completed, and cancelled. On the CAPA list and form those states appear as مسودة, مُعيّن, قيد التنفيذ, مكتمل, and ملغي. Delayed actions are flagged. Dashboard cards use the English CAPA labels below. The CAPA list and form in this edition use Arabic labels.
📋 Audit plans, tasks, and indicators Audit Management includes audit plans, tasks, and indicators. An indicator stores a category, baseline, target, and current value. This is record keeping. It does not execute control tests.
🌍 Arabic and English Audit operations, review results, audit plans, tasks, indicators, and the executive dashboard use English source text and ship with an Arabic translation catalog. Some report, portal, and search labels remain English in Arabic. Corrective Action (CAPA) list, form, and search labels in this edition are Arabic.

Executive dashboard

The Executive Dashboard shows the KPI cards below. Filters are Reviewer, Entity under review, Execution status, and Risk level. They apply to the KPI totals and the selected metric chart. Execution status filters review-result status. Risk level filters review-result severity (Low, Medium, High, and High Risk). These filters do not select audit operation states or filter the computed Risk Rating.

Audit operation workflow

Each audit operation has six execution statuses. The usual path runs from Draft through Closed. Cancelled is available until the operation is already cancelled, and Reset to Draft is available from every status except Draft.

StateWhat this status is for
DraftSet the entity under review, audit team, lead reviewer, dates, scope, and objectives. Start Fieldwork moves the operation forward.
FieldworkRecord review results and attachments while the operation is in progress. This status does not run control tests. Move to Reporting continues the path.
ReportingCheck report readiness and use Issue Report. Failed checks open the warning wizard.
IssuedThe operation report can be printed. Management responses remain on the review results. Close is available from this status.
ClosedThe audit operation is marked closed.
CancelledThe audit operation is marked cancelled.

Questions teams ask

Is this a complete GRC platform?

No. AuditFlow Standard is for internal audit operations, review results, readiness checks, corrective actions, and operational KPI cards inside Odoo.

Can we use it in production?

Yes, after you validate the process, access rules, translations, and training on a staging database.

What about Arabic and English?

Audit operations, review results, audit plans, tasks, indicators, and the executive dashboard use English source text and ship with an Arabic translation catalog. Some report, portal, and search labels remain English in Arabic. Corrective Action (CAPA) list, form, and search labels in this edition are Arabic. Confirm both languages during implementation.
